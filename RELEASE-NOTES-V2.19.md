# Adaptive GLB Viewer V2.19

## Contact and directional shadows
- Dark compact contact core directly beneath the model.
- Softer low-opacity local falloff around the contact area.
- Directional key shadow now fades progressively across the ground.
- Shadow fade radius automatically scales to the fitted model.
- Contact shadow tint is darker and remains compatible with selectable studio colours.
- Clearer contact-core, near-falloff, and far-directional control labels.

## Navigation
- Object-centred orbit remains the default.
- Right-drag panning is restored for repositioning a zoomed model.
- Two-finger touch panning is available on mobile devices.
- Reset View restores the exact fitted model centre.

## Preserved
- V2.18 responsive sidebar framing, top-positioned adaptive resolution control, optical materials, HDRIs, effects, PWA files, and performance controls.
